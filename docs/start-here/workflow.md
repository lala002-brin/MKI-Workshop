<h2>
Computational Methods
</h2>


<p class="section-intro">
Different computational approaches are used to investigate materials
from electronic, atomic, and data-driven perspectives.
</p>



<div class="method-grid">


<div class="method-card">


<h3>
Quantum Simulation
</h3>


<p>
Study electronic structures and fundamental properties of materials
using quantum mechanical approaches.
</p>


<div class="method-detail">

<strong>
Focus
</strong>

<p>
Electronic structure, energy calculation, and material properties.
</p>

</div>


</div>



<div class="method-card">


<h3>
Atomistic Simulation
</h3>


<p>
Explore atomic interactions, material behavior, and structural
evolution at the atomic scale.
</p>


<div class="method-detail">

<strong>
Focus
</strong>

<p>
Atomic dynamics, stability, and molecular behavior.
</p>

</div>


</div>



<div class="method-card">


<h3>
AI Acceleration
</h3>


<p>
Apply data-driven approaches to accelerate material prediction
and discovery processes.
</p>


<div class="method-detail">

<strong>
Focus
</strong>

<p>
Machine learning, prediction models, and materials screening.
</p>

</div>
