    Research Progression

    The projects move from fundamental electronic-structure calculations toward increasingly complex atomistic systems and transport analysis.

    01

    ELECTRONIC STRUCTURE

    Graphene

    Start with a well-defined two-dimensional material and examine its electronic structure through first-principles calculations.

    02

    ELECTROLYTE SYSTEM

    LiTFSI–EMIM/TFSI Electrolyte Transport

    Move from a crystalline material toward a more complex electrolyte system and atomistic simulation.

    03

    INTERFACE MODELING

    Graphene–Ionic Liquid Interface

    Extend the computational system to a material interface and examine structural and dynamical behavior across different material components.

    04

    ATOMISTIC TRANSPORT

    Battery Electrolyte

    Investigate molecular transport, structural dynamics, and diffusion behavior through atomistic simulation and trajectory analysis.

    Computational Research Workflow

    Every project follows a common research workflow. The computational method changes according to the material system and scientific question.

    01

    Define

    Identify the scientific question, material system, and properties that need to be investigated.

    02

    Build

    Prepare atomic structures, simulation cells, input parameters, and the required computational representation.

    03

    Simulate

    Run the appropriate first-principles or atomistic calculation using the available computational environment.

    04

    Analyze

    Process numerical outputs, trajectories, and calculated properties using suitable analysis tools.

    05

    Interpret

    Relate the computational results to the original research question and identify meaningful scientific observations.

    SCIENTIFIC QUESTIONS

    From Method to Scientific Question

    Each computational method addresses a different scientific question. The material system determines the appropriate level of simulation and analysis.

    01

    ELECTRONIC STRUCTURE

    What are the electronic properties?

    First-principles calculations can provide electronic-structure information such as density of states and band structure.

    Quantum ESPRESSO · DFT
    02

    ATOMISTIC BEHAVIOR

    How do atoms and molecules evolve?

    Atomistic simulations provide trajectories that can be examined to understand structural and dynamical behavior.

    DC-DFTB-MD · Molecular Dynamics
    03

    INTERFACE

    How does a material interact with its environment?

    Interface models allow structural and dynamical behavior to be studied across different material components.

    Interface Modeling · LAMMPS
    04

    TRANSPORT

    How do particles move through the system?

    Trajectory analysis can reveal diffusion, molecular organization, and transport behavior within complex material systems.

    MSD · RDF · Diffusion Analysis
    05

    AI ACCELERATION

    How can simulation be extended to larger systems?

    Machine-learning potentials can accelerate atomistic simulations while retaining a connection to first-principles data.

    MACE · Machine Learning · HPC
