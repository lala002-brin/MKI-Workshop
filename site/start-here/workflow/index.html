    Computational Workflow

    From Scientific Questions to Materials Discovery

    A structured pathway that transforms scientific problems into computational models, simulation workflows, and materials insights.

    Research Journey

    Computational materials science follows a systematic process that connects scientific questions, computational methods, and research discoveries.

    01

    Research Question

    Define scientific problems and research objectives that guide computational investigation.

    02

    Material Modeling

    Translate real materials into computational representations for simulation.

    03

    Computational Simulation

    Apply computational methods to investigate material structures and properties.

    04

    Data Analysis

    Interpret simulation results and extract meaningful scientific information.

    05

    Materials Discovery

    Use computational knowledge to evaluate and design potential materials.

    Computational Methods

    Different computational approaches are used to investigate materials from electronic, atomic, and data-driven perspectives.

    Quantum Simulation

    Study electronic structures and fundamental properties of materials using quantum mechanical approaches.

    Focus

    Electronic structure, energy calculation, and material properties.

    Atomistic Simulation

    Explore atomic interactions, material behavior, and structural evolution at the atomic scale.

    Focus

    Atomic dynamics, stability, and molecular behavior.

    AI Acceleration

    Apply data-driven approaches to accelerate material prediction and discovery processes.

    Focus

    Machine learning, prediction models, and materials screening.
