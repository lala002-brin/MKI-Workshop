## Computational Research Workflow

<p class="section-description">
Every project follows a common research workflow. The computational
method changes according to the material system and scientific question.
</p>


<div class="workflow-container">


<div class="workflow-item">

<div class="workflow-number">
01
</div>

<h3>
Define
</h3>

<p>
Identify the scientific question, material system, and
properties that need to be investigated.
</p>

</div>


<div class="workflow-item">

<div class="workflow-number">
02
</div>

<h3>
Build
</h3>

<p>
Prepare atomic structures, simulation cells, input parameters,
and the required computational representation.
</p>

</div>


<div class="workflow-item">

<div class="workflow-number">
03
</div>

<h3>
Simulate
</h3>

<p>
Run the appropriate first-principles or atomistic calculation
using the available computational environment.
</p>

</div>


<div class="workflow-item">

<div class="workflow-number">
04
</div>

<h3>
Analyze
</h3>

<p>
Process numerical outputs, trajectories, and calculated
properties using suitable analysis tools.
</p>

</div>


<div class="workflow-item">

<div class="workflow-number">
05
</div>

<h3>
Interpret
</h3>

<p>
Relate the computational results to the original research
question and identify meaningful scientific observations.
</p>

</div>


</div>

<div class="research-questions">

<div class="research-question-header">

<p class="question-eyebrow">
SCIENTIFIC QUESTIONS
</p>

<h2>
From Method to Scientific Question
</h2>

<p>
Each computational method addresses a different scientific question.
The material system determines the appropriate level of simulation
and analysis.
</p>

</div>


<div class="question-list">


<div class="question-item">

<div class="question-index">
01
</div>

<div class="question-body">

<p class="question-category">
ELECTRONIC STRUCTURE
</p>

<h3>
What are the electronic properties?
</h3>

<p>
First-principles calculations can provide electronic-structure
information such as density of states and band structure.
</p>

<div class="question-method">
Quantum ESPRESSO · DFT
</div>

</div>

</div>



<div class="question-item">

<div class="question-index">
02
</div>

<div class="question-body">

<p class="question-category">
ATOMISTIC BEHAVIOR
</p>

<h3>
How do atoms and molecules evolve?
</h3>

<p>
Atomistic simulations provide trajectories that can be examined
to understand structural and dynamical behavior.
</p>

<div class="question-method">
DC-DFTB-MD · Molecular Dynamics
</div>

</div>

</div>



<div class="question-item">

<div class="question-index">
03
</div>

<div class="question-body">

<p class="question-category">
INTERFACE
</p>

<h3>
How does a material interact with its environment?
</h3>

<p>
Interface models allow structural and dynamical behavior to be
studied across different material components.
</p>

<div class="question-method">
Interface Modeling · LAMMPS
</div>

</div>

</div>



<div class="question-item">

<div class="question-index">
04
</div>

<div class="question-body">

<p class="question-category">
TRANSPORT
</p>

<h3>
How do particles move through the system?
</h3>

<p>
Trajectory analysis can reveal diffusion, molecular organization,
and transport behavior within complex material systems.
</p>

<div class="question-method">
MSD · RDF · Diffusion Analysis
</div>

</div>

</div>



<div class="question-item">

<div class="question-index">
05
</div>

<div class="question-body">

<p class="question-category">
AI ACCELERATION
</p>

<h3>
How can simulation be extended to larger systems?
</h3>

<p>
Machine-learning potentials can accelerate atomistic simulations
while retaining a connection to first-principles data.
</p>

<div class="question-method">
MACE · Machine Learning · HPC
</div>

</div>

</div>
