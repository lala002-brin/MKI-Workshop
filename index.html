    Hello, curious mind!

    A little lab for a whole lot of questions.

    KITCAT LAB is a growing learning and exploration space for computational chemistry and scientific computing. Here, concepts meet hands-on experiments, useful software, and practical tools.

    Whether you are taking your first steps in DFT or learning how to interpret a molecular simulation, the goal is the same: understand what you compute, why you compute it, and what the results really mean.

    The little story behind the name

    Why KITCAT?

    Because curiosity sometimes starts with a cat. KITCAT LAB takes its name from a much-loved cat and turns that small spark of inspiration into a space for serious questions, playful exploration, and collaborative learning.

    Start exploring ↗

    Built together

    Small team. Big curiosity.

    KITCAT LAB is a collaborative space shaped by the people who write, test, teach, question, and improve its learning materials and tools. It is not an official website of any computational software project.

    Names, roles, and contributions can be added here when the team profile is ready. We'd rather introduce people accurately than invent affiliations or project credits.
