# Workshop Guide


## Computational Materials Science Workshop

## First-Principles, HPC & AI for Materials Discovery


Welcome to the Computational Materials Science Workshop.


This guide provides an overview of the workshop structure, learning objectives, computational tools, and expected outcomes.



---

# Workshop Objectives


This workshop aims to introduce participants to modern computational approaches for materials discovery by combining:


- First-Principles Calculation
- High Performance Computing
- Atomistic Simulation
- Artificial Intelligence



Participants will learn how computational methods can be applied to understand material structures and properties at the atomic scale.



---

# Workshop Structure


The workshop is organized into several computational stages:


## Stage 1

### Computational Materials Science Foundation


Understanding:

- Materials modeling concepts
- Computational approaches
- Structure-property relationships



## Stage 2

### First-Principles Calculation


Using:

**Quantum ESPRESSO**


Topics:

- Density Functional Theory
- Geometry optimization
- Electronic structure analysis



## Stage 3

### Accelerated Simulation


Using:

**DFTB+**


Topics:

- Efficient atomistic simulation
- Large-scale material modeling



## Stage 4

### AI Accelerated Materials Discovery


Using:

**Machine Learning Potential**


Topics:

- Machine learning models
- Atomic simulation acceleration



## Stage 5

### High Performance Computing


Topics:

- Linux environment
- HPC workflow
- Parallel computation
