<div class="foundation-intro">

<h2>
Why Computational Materials Science?
</h2>

<p>
Materials research combines scientific understanding with
computational methods to investigate structures, properties,
and interactions at scales that can be difficult to access
through experiments alone.
</p>

<p>
Computational materials science provides a framework for translating
scientific questions into models that can be evaluated through
simulation and data analysis.
</p>

</div>
