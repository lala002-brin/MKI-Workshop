    Your Journey Into
    Computational Materials
    Science

    MK1 × ITB × BRIN Computational Materials Science Workshop

    introduces participants to modern computational approaches for understanding, modeling, and discovering advanced materials.

    This workshop provides a structured learning pathway that connects scientific fundamentals, computational methods, simulation techniques, high-performance computing, and artificial intelligence for materials research.

    Participants will learn how scientific questions are transformed into computational models, evaluated through simulations, and interpreted to obtain meaningful scientific insights.

    About This Workshop

    Computational Materials Science is an interdisciplinary field that combines physics, chemistry, materials science, and computational methods to understand and design advanced materials.

    🔬 Scientific Foundation

    Connect fundamental scientific concepts with computational approaches to study material structures, properties, and interactions.

    💻 Computational Approach

    Learn how simulation methods, high-performance computing, and artificial intelligence support modern materials research.

    🚀 Research Application

    Explore how computational workflows are applied to solve scientific problems and accelerate materials discovery.

    Learning Philosophy

    The workshop follows a structured learning approach that connects scientific understanding, computational practice, and research application.

    🧠

    Understand

    Build fundamental knowledge of materials science, computational concepts, and simulation principles.

    ⚙️

    Simulate

    Apply computational methods to construct models, perform calculations, and explore material behavior.

    🔍

    Analyze

    Interpret simulation results and extract scientific insights for materials research.

    Workshop Roadmap

    The workshop follows a progressive pathway that guides participants from fundamental concepts to advanced computational research applications.

    01

    Foundation

    Build fundamental knowledge required for computational materials science, including materials concepts and scientific principles.

    • Computational Materials Science fundamentals
    • Materials structures and properties
    • Introduction to Density Functional Theory
    02

    First-Principles Simulation

    Understand materials properties through quantum mechanical calculations and electronic structure analysis.

    • First-Principles methods
    • Density Functional Theory
    • Electronic structure analysis
    03

    System Design

    Prepare computational models through structural construction and simulation setup.

    • Material modeling
    • Structure optimization
    • Computational workflow preparation
    04

    Atomistic Simulation

    Explore atomic-scale behavior using molecular simulation approaches.

    • Molecular dynamics
    • Atomic interactions
    • Material behavior analysis
    05

    HPC Environment

    Learn computational infrastructure for large-scale simulations.

    • High Performance Computing
    • Parallel computing
    • Simulation execution
    06

    AI Acceleration

    Explore machine learning approaches for accelerating materials discovery.

    • Machine Learning
    • Artificial Intelligence
    • Data-driven discovery
    07

    Research Application

    Apply computational methods to real scientific research problems.

    Recommended Learning Path

    Participants are recommended to follow the learning sequence to gradually develop computational materials science skills.

    01

    Build Foundation

    Understand fundamental concepts in materials science, physics, chemistry, and computational methods.

    02

    Learn Simulation

    Explore first-principles calculations, molecular simulation, and computational workflows.

    03

    Analyze Results

    Interpret simulation outputs and connect computational results with scientific questions.

    04

    Explore Research

    Apply computational approaches to advanced materials research and discovery.

    For Experienced Users

    Participants with previous experience in computational science or materials research may explore specific modules according to their research objectives.

    ⚛️

    Computational Methods

    Explore specific simulation methods, theoretical approaches, and computational techniques.

    🔬

    Research Workflows

    Review complete workflows from system preparation, simulation, and analysis.

    🚀

    Advanced Applications

    Explore HPC, artificial intelligence, and advanced research applications.

    Before Starting the Workshop

    Before starting the workshop, participants are encouraged to prepare basic scientific knowledge and computational skills.

    🧪 Scientific Background

    Basic understanding of:

    • Physics
    • Chemistry
    • Materials Science

    💻 Computational Skills

    Basic familiarity with:

    • Linux command line
    • Scientific computing
    • Data analysis workflow

    🧠 Learning Mindset

    Participants are encouraged to:

    • Explore computational methods
    • Practice simulation workflows
    • Connect results with science

    Expected Outcomes

    After completing this workshop, participants are expected to understand the complete computational materials science workflow, from defining research questions to analyzing simulation results.

    🧠 Fundamental Understanding

    Understand fundamental concepts in computational materials science, including scientific principles and simulation approaches.

    🔬 Computational Methods

    Identify suitable computational approaches based on scientific questions and research objectives.

    🧱 System Preparation

    Prepare material structures and computational models for simulation workflows.

    📊 Data Analysis

    Analyze simulation outputs and extract meaningful scientific information from computational results.

    💻 HPC Workflow

    Understand how high-performance computing supports large-scale materials simulations.

    🤖 AI Materials Discovery

    Explore artificial intelligence approaches for accelerating modern materials discovery.
