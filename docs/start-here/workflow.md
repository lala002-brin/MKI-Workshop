# Computational Workflow


## From Material Question to Scientific Discovery

Computational materials science follows a systematic workflow that
connects scientific questions with computational models and
simulation results.

The workflow begins with material design and system preparation,
followed by quantum simulations, atomistic modeling, data analysis,
and advanced acceleration using high-performance computing and
artificial intelligence.

# Workflow Stages

## 01 Research Question

Every computational materials study begins with a scientific question
that defines the objective of the simulation.

The research question determines:

- what material system will be studied
- which computational method is required
- what properties need to be analyzed

Examples:

- Understanding electronic properties
- Investigating atomic interactions
- Predicting material behavior


---

## 02 Material Design and System Preparation

A computational model is constructed according to the research objective.

This stage converts a scientific problem into a simulation-ready system.

Activities include:

- molecular construction
- crystal structure preparation
- interface modeling
- simulation parameter preparation


---

## 03 First-Principles Simulation

First-principles calculations describe material properties using
quantum mechanical approaches.

This stage provides fundamental information about electronic structures
and energetic properties.

Topics:

- Density Functional Theory (DFT)
- electronic structure calculations
- geometry optimization
- energy evaluation


---

## 04 Atomistic Simulation

Atomistic simulations investigate material behavior at the atomic scale.

These methods describe how atoms and molecules interact over time.

Topics:

- Molecular Dynamics
- molecular interactions
- structural evolution
- transport properties


---

## 05 Data Analysis

Simulation results are processed to extract meaningful scientific
information.

Analysis includes:

- structural properties
- electronic properties
- molecular behavior
- diffusion characteristics


---

## 06 High Performance Computing

Large-scale computational materials simulations require efficient
computational resources.

HPC enables researchers to perform complex calculations through:

- parallel computation
- large-scale simulations
- optimized job execution
- computational workflow management


---

## 07 Artificial Intelligence Acceleration

Artificial intelligence provides data-driven approaches to accelerate
materials simulation and discovery.

Applications include:

- machine learning potential
- surrogate models
- AI-assisted prediction
- accelerated materials discovery
