## About This Workshop


Computational Materials Science is an interdisciplinary field that
combines physics, chemistry, mathematics, and computer science to study
materials through computational approaches.


Modern materials research requires an understanding of the relationship
between atomic structures, electronic properties, and material
performance.


Through computational methods, researchers can investigate materials at
different scales, from electronic structures to atomic interactions,
and develop predictions that support materials discovery.


This workshop introduces a complete computational pathway, starting
from fundamental concepts, computational model preparation, simulation
methods, data analysis, and advanced research applications.
